NINTENDO SWITCH GAMING GUIDE

How to Choose the Best Nintendo Switch Games for Different Play Styles

Discover how to choose Nintendo Switch games based on your preferred genre, gaming habits, available time, and the type of experience you enjoy most.

The Nintendo Switch has something that many gaming platforms struggle to offer: variety without making the experience complicated. You can pick up the console for a quick ten-minute session, spend an entire weekend playing an RPG, or invite friends over for a multiplayer game.

With such a large library, however, finding the right game can sometimes be harder than actually playing it. There are hundreds of interesting titles across different genres, and every player has different preferences.

Understanding your own play style is therefore one of the easiest ways to find Nintendo Switch games that you will genuinely enjoy.

Start by Understanding Your Gaming Style

Before choosing a new Nintendo Switch game, think about what you actually enjoy when playing.

Do you like spending hours exploring a large world? Do you prefer short matches that you can finish quickly? Maybe you enjoy solving puzzles, building characters, competing against other players, or simply relaxing after a long day.

There is no correct answer.

The best game for one player may be completely uninteresting to another. That's why genre alone isn't always enough when deciding what to play.

Games for Players Who Love Exploration

01

Explore, Discover and Wander

Exploration-focused games are ideal for players who enjoy discovering new locations and deciding where to go next.

These games often provide large environments, hidden areas, optional objectives, collectibles, and secrets.

Instead of moving from one level to another in a completely linear way, players may have more freedom to explore.

The appeal comes from curiosity. You never quite know what you will find around the next corner.

If exploration is your favorite part of gaming, look for Nintendo Switch titles that emphasize open environments, discovery, environmental storytelling, and optional side activities.

RPGs for Long Gaming Sessions

02

Character Progression and Long Adventures

Role-playing games are a natural choice for players who enjoy long-term progression.

RPGs often allow you to develop characters, unlock abilities, collect equipment, complete quests, and gradually become stronger.

Some games can take dozens of hours to complete, while others provide even more content through side quests and additional activities.

The Switch is particularly convenient for this style of gaming because you can play an RPG in handheld mode whenever you have some free time.

Even if you only complete one quest during a short session, you can put the console down and continue your adventure later.

Fast-Paced Games for Action Fans

03

Immediate Action and Challenging Gameplay

Some players don't want slow exploration or long conversations. They want immediate action.

Action games can be a great choice for players who enjoy quick reactions, combat, movement, and constant challenges.

Depending on the game, this can include anything from straightforward arcade-style gameplay to highly demanding combat systems.

Difficulty can also be an important factor. Some action games are designed to be accessible to beginners, while others expect players to learn enemy patterns and master precise controls.

If you enjoy improving through practice, challenging action games can provide a very satisfying experience.

Racing Games for Quick Sessions

04

Easy to Start and Easy to Enjoy

Racing games are particularly convenient when you don't have much time.

You can start a race, finish it within a few minutes, and stop playing without worrying about losing progress in a complicated storyline.

They are also excellent for multiplayer sessions.

A racing game can work equally well when you're playing alone or when friends are sitting together with their own controllers.

This makes the genre one of the easiest choices for casual gaming sessions.

Relaxing Games for a Slower Experience

05

Gaming Without the Pressure

Not every gaming session needs to involve combat or competition.

Simulation, farming, life-management, and puzzle games can provide a much calmer experience.

These games are often designed around gradual progress rather than constant pressure.

They can be especially enjoyable when you want something to play while relaxing.

Instead of worrying about difficult bosses or competitive rankings, you might spend your time building a virtual environment, managing resources, completing simple objectives, or solving puzzles.

Multiplayer Games for Friends and Family

The Nintendo Switch has always been a strong platform for social gaming.

Local multiplayer allows several people to play together in the same room, while online multiplayer provides opportunities to compete or cooperate with players elsewhere.

Party games, sports titles, fighting games, and racing games are obvious choices, but many other genres also include multiplayer features.

When choosing a multiplayer game, it is worth checking the number of supported players and whether local or online modes are available.

Don't Ignore Indie Nintendo Switch Games

One of the easiest mistakes a player can make is focusing exclusively on major releases.

Nintendo Switch has an enormous selection of indie games, and many of them offer gameplay concepts that feel completely different from mainstream releases.

Independent developers often experiment with unusual mechanics, visual styles, storytelling techniques, and level design.

Some indie games can also be completed in a relatively short amount of time, making them ideal for players who don't want to commit to a 50-hour adventure.

Finding New Games Through Gaming Resources

With so many games available, discovering something new can take time.

Instead of searching randomly, it can help to use gaming resources that organize information around specific platforms, genres, and titles.

Consider How Much Time You Have

Your available gaming time should influence your choice.

If you only have a few minutes each day, a game based around short levels or matches may be more enjoyable than a massive RPG.

If you have several hours available on weekends, a story-driven adventure or RPG could be a better fit.

This sounds obvious, but it can make a surprisingly big difference.

A game becomes much more enjoyable when its structure matches your lifestyle.

Try Different Genres

Another good way to improve your Nintendo Switch library is to avoid playing the same type of game all the time.

If you normally play action games, try a puzzle title. If you spend most of your time playing RPGs, experiment with an indie platformer.

Someone who normally plays alone might also enjoy trying a local multiplayer game.

You don't have to become a fan of every genre.

The goal is simply to discover what works for you. Sometimes a game from a genre you normally ignore can become one of your favorite experiences.

Check Reviews Before Starting a New Game

Reviews can also help when deciding between several titles.

Pay attention to comments about gameplay, performance, controls, difficulty, length, and replayability rather than focusing only on numerical scores.

A game receiving an extremely high rating doesn't automatically mean it is right for you. Personal preference should always come first.

For example, a difficult action game may receive excellent reviews but still be frustrating if you prefer relaxed gameplay.

Similarly, a slow simulation game might be perfect for one person and boring for someone looking for constant action.

Build a Balanced Switch Library

A good Nintendo Switch library doesn't have to contain dozens of games from the same genre.

A balanced collection could include an RPG for long sessions, a racing game for quick matches, an action title for challenging gameplay, an indie game for something different, and a relaxing game for quieter evenings.

This variety gives you something to play regardless of your mood.

Sometimes you'll want a serious adventure. Other times, you'll simply want to start a quick race and put the console down.

Final Thoughts

Choosing Nintendo Switch games becomes much easier when you understand your own play style.

Exploration fans can look for games with large environments and secrets. RPG players can choose experiences built around character progression and long stories. Action fans can search for challenging combat, while racing and multiplayer games are perfect for shorter or social sessions.

Indie games are also worth exploring because they can introduce creative ideas that aren't always found in major releases.

Ultimately, there is no single “best” Nintendo Switch game for everyone. The best choice is the game that fits your interests, available time, and preferred way of playing.

With such a diverse library, the fun is not only in playing games but also in discovering the next title that might become your new favorite.
